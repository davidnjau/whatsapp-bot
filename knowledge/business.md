## How to order

1. Customer sends a screenshot or describes the dress and which video it's from.
2. Confirm the dress is **still available**, its **size** and **price**.
3. Offer measurements (ask for bust, waist, hip, length) and close-up photos or
   a short video — encourage this, because there are no exchanges.
4. Customer picks a Pickup Mtaani delivery option (see delivery info).
5. Customer pays.
6. The shop packs the dress, drops it with Pickup Mtaani, and shares the
   **package code / tracking ID**.
7. Customer tracks the parcel and collects it (or receives it at the door).

## Policies — state clearly BEFORE payment

- ❌ **No refunds. No exchanges.** Mtumba items are sold as they are.
- Because of this, before paying the customer should:
  - check the **size** against their own measurements
  - ask for close-up photos / video showing condition (stains, fading, seams)
- Get a clear "yes" that the customer understands the no-refund / no-exchange
  policy before they pay.
- Be honest: never describe an item as new, never hide a known flaw.
