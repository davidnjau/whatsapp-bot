# Delivery — Pickup Mtaani

Dress Nation delivers countrywide through **Pickup Mtaani**, a Kenyan parcel
network: the shop drops the parcel at an agent, the buyer collects nearby or
gets doorstep delivery.

## Options to offer

| Option | What happens | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Mtaani (agent)** | Shop drops at an agent; buyer collects from their nearest agent | Cheapest — the default for most customers |
| **Doorstep** | A rider delivers to home/office; pay on delivery possible | Convenience |
| **Errands** | Parcel goes via a matatu SACCO or courier the buyer picks (e.g. Easy Coach, 2NK) | Upcountry buyers with no nearby agent |

How to recommend:
- Nairobi / near an agent → **Mtaani agent** (cheapest).
- Wants door delivery → **Doorstep**.
- Upcountry with no agent nearby → **Errands** via their preferred SACCO.
- Always ask the customer's **area/town** and nearest agent (or doorstep
  location) so the fee can be confirmed. **Fees come only from Pickup Mtaani —
  never guess.** If you can't look it up, the shop will confirm it.

## Timing (give ranges, not promises)

Riders leave the CBD in two shifts daily: 10am–3pm and 3pm–7pm.

- Dropped before 10am → usually same day, 3pm–7pm
- Dropped at an agent 10am–4pm → collected that evening; delivered next day 10am–3pm
- Dropped after 2pm → generally next day
- Dropped at an agent after 5pm → following day, 3pm–7pm

Exact timing for afternoon drop-offs can vary — give a range and never promise
an exact time.

## Tracking

- Live tracking with alerts. Anyone can track on the Pickup Mtaani website
  using the **package code / tracking ID**.
- The shop shares the package code once the parcel is dropped off.

## Collection — tell every agent-delivery customer

Collect within **3 days**. After 3 days the parcel goes back to Pickup
Mtaani's main office (7 more days to collect, then a final 3 days). If still
uncollected, Pickup Mtaani may **dispose of the parcel** (about 13 days after
the first deadline). Since Dress Nation has no refunds, an uncollected parcel
means the money is lost — remind customers to collect promptly.

## Damaged or tampered parcels (Pickup Mtaani's process)

This is Pickup Mtaani's delivery-problem process — separate from the shop's
no-refund policy:

- Report damage/tampering **while still at the pickup point** (DM, WhatsApp or
  call to Pickup Mtaani) **with a photo**.
- A refund claim from Pickup Mtaani needs: the M-Pesa statements (seller and
  buyer) **and** a picture of the product as listed on the seller's page.
- Claims missing these, or showing signs of editing, can be declined.

**Advise every customer: open and check the parcel at the agent before
leaving.** If something is wrong — photograph it there, report to Pickup
Mtaani immediately, and tell Dress Nation on 0725571239.

## What they carry

Clothing (dresses ✓), shoes, cosmetics — non-perishable, non-bulky items.
Not accepted: money; food/flowers/groceries; flammables or ammunition;
electronics; pets/ashes/human remains; bulky or heavy items. Fragile items
travel at the sender's risk.
